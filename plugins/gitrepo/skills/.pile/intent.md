The skills that send an agent to `pile`, the multipile command line, at the moment each of its workflows applies: they hold no workflow and no reference page of their own.

When a skill applies, which `pile` commands it has the agent run, and what the agent does where `pile` is not installed belong here. So does what a skill adds around a workflow: how a review finds its base and its task, and where its findings are posted. A step of a workflow does not belong here, nor what a pile, an intent or a link is: `pile guide` says those. How the skills are packaged, versioned or installed does not belong here either.

Read this pile to find out what makes an agent reach for `pile`, and with which command. Change it when `pile guide` gains, renames or drops a workflow, or an agent misses the moment a workflow applies.
